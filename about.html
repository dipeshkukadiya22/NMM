About NMM

If Nimbu Mirchi Media Solutions had a rupee for every time someone said, “Whoa, that’s impressive experience!” we’d still be making content—just with slightly fancier coffee.

Between the founders, they bring over 20 years of combined experience, having directed some of Indian television’s biggest reality bangers and lived through every form of youth content, from six-second vines to sprawling fictional universes. Sounds familiar? We know.

But what truly sets them apart is their hunger. Not the feed-them-a-thali kind, but the give-them-a-crazy-brief-and-watch-them-run-wild kind. That drive led them to ditch the big comfy chairs at fancy TV networks in 2014 and start something spicier, tangier, and infinitely more fun—Nimbu Mirchi Media Solutions.

Since then, the team has been busy adding zest to screens, from madcap digital sketches to slick branded content. We don’t just think outside the box—they repurpose it, paint it yellow, and set it on fire (creatively, of course). Want proof? Our portfolio speaks louder than words, stacked with enough trumpets to start a marching band.
