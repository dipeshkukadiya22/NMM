About NMM

If we had a rupee for every time someone said, “Whoa, that’s impressive experience!” we’d still be making content, just with slightly fancier coffee.

Between the two of us, we’ve clocked over 20 years of combined experience, directed some of Indian television’s biggest reality bangers, and basically lived every form of youth content, from six-second vines to full-blown fictional universes. Sounds familiar? We know.

But here’s what actually makes us tick: we’re HUNGRY. Not just the feed-us-a-thali kind of hungry, but the give-us-a-crazy-brief-and-watch-us-run-wild kind. That hunger led us to ditch the big comfy chairs at fancy TV networks in 2014 and start something spicier, tangier, and way more fun – Nimbu Mirchi Media Solutions.

Since then, we’ve been busy adding zest to screens, from madcap digital sketches to slick branded content. We don’t just think outside the box, we repurpose it, paint it yellow, and set it on fire (creatively, of course). Want proof? Check out our work history. We’ve got enough trumpets to start a marching band and we’re always game if you want to blow one with us.
